# Using it

A short tour for the people who watch. Admins also see **Admin settings** under the gear icon.

## Signing in

Your name and password. If an admin gave you a temporary password, you are asked to choose your own first.
Change it any time from the gear icon > **Change password**.

## Finding channels

- **Manage** is where you choose which channel groups you want. Tick groups and press **Save groups**.
  **Select shown** and **Clear shown** work on whatever the filter box is currently showing.
- The group drop-down lists your groups. **Reorder** puts them in the order you like.
- **Search** looks across your groups.
- Tap the star on a channel to add it to **Favorites**. The newest favorite comes first.

## The guide

Each channel row shows what is on across about three hours. Scroll sideways for later; the red line is now.
The channel name stays in view on the left, and the title of whatever is on now stays readable as you
scroll. Tap anywhere on the row to watch the channel. Rows keep the same height in every mode.

Guide data comes from your provider and from any guide sources an admin has added. A channel with no
listings says "No guide data for this channel".

## Making it yours

Press **Edit** above the list. Rows then show buttons instead of the guide:

- **Top**, **▲**, **▼** move the channel inside the group (or inside Favorites).
- **Hide** takes the channel out of your list (in Favorites it is **Remove**). **Hidden** lists what you
  hid in this group so you can bring things back with **Show**.
- **Reset order** returns this group to the provider's order.

Only you see these changes, and they follow you to every device you sign in on. Press **Edit** again when done.

## Watching

Tap a channel to play. **Stop** ends the stream and frees your provider connection. If the provider's
connection limit is reached you are told who is watching and can choose **Watch anyway**. **Stop all
streams now** in Settings ends everything the server is pulling.

## Admins: fixing a missing guide

In **Edit** mode, an admin can tap a channel's name. The sheet says what the guide matched (or that it found
nothing) and what is on now. Search for another channel that does have listings and press **Use** to copy its
guide. **Stop using a copied guide** goes back to automatic matching. This applies for everyone and is saved
in `epgmap.json`.
